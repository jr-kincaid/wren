## Narration

You awake from the Sleepless night to what appears to be fireworks. Outside your window you can see spell casters figures hovering mid-air around city scape of Wren. They are casting spells to attack the walking dead that now seem to roam the street.

There is also two weather disturbances. Snow drops in heavy flakes from the sky covering everything in slush. A strange event for autumn. Also most of the body of the each undead is obscured by a mist that covers every in inch of the ground.

Your window is coated in frost. You note the Palazzo court yard does not contain any of the undead.You seem safe for now. While all these details are a bit strange. A silver dragon also hovers in the sky. It too is casting spells while being the size of a two story building.
