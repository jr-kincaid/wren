## DM Note

The most emotion inducing thing about being a DM is progression of time. Is the game dying or growing. In what direction will be most pleasing or precarious for the welfare of the game. What keeps players engaged. Really chasing that feeling of the mists of past gaming memories.

One key bit of nostalgia for me, was as a Storyteller of White Wolf Second Edition Vampire game. There was something magical about the venue of Campaign Headquarters. The head winds of life would lift a player from our game. Like a force of fate- in would blow more players. In fact at points, I had to turn people away. There was a quality of player the troupe would pursue and cultivate.

The other aspect of game mastering within the World of Darkness was progression of the Architecture of the Campaign and the ad hoc scenes we would play within that Framework. Emphasis would shift as folks left, missed a session, or were there to watch the spectacle unfold in other players movements, exposition, and outcomes.

One thing that I keep coming back to as a game master that I cannot emphasize enough. I appreciate players that focus on listening and what their character needs to do to impact the story. Often times, players think its helpful to point out how to make another player more effective. I think for the health of the game, the space for this is during breaks or outside of a scene or encounter. While a game in scene and especially when we are going round by round, Meta Gaming, is damaging to immersion and tempo.

I will include some exercises during Session 2:

1. **Mechanics Discussion** - Questions about my character, mechanics, none roleplaying things. We will go player by player and have discussion about how this character could function better in the group. During breaks and space provided like this we should have these discussion. While we are in scene these types of discussions should be limited. I will tend to make a quick ruling and move on. If we later decides there is best way to handle something in the future. This will allow us to evolve as a troupe while not slugging the health of a scene.
2. **Focus Polls** - As an example, I will have a new Next Time Campaign Focus poll at minimum advertised. I will list general descriptions for scenes or arcs, maybe four or five. Interest will decide what I focus on developing for future sessions. Check out Polls for an example, and please vote on all polls, as you can. There will probably be a handful I am inspired to hold, as time goes on. This is to make players a part of planning. It is also to inform generally where the campaign is likely heading. Level editing in the live will be of a different quality than something I can plot and weed in the off times. While Serendipity is cool, decision fatigue is also very real after a few hours of play.
3. **The Parking Lot** - I will hand out a pad of Stickies to each player. Your job is to write down anything you would like to discuss with me (Hand to me), other players, or the group (slap on the Public Parking Lot space on the wall). Ideas and questions are always good, but maybe not urgent. There will always be time to discuss these provided. Write them down so they do not slip from your brain into ether.
4. **Wren Flavor** - I will provide a sketch or an enhancement of details of the world around your characters somewhere in session. Probably several interestings sprinkled in!
5. **Debrief** - We will end by Triage the Public Parking Lot to gather action items and discussion. We will also have a Feedback sandwich.
   1. What went great?
   2. What did you not like?
   3. What can we improve or change.
6. **Post Session Breakouts** - I will leave slowly if you need time thats a great time to book me. Also we can do voice or text on Discord during the off time.
