## Campaign Description

**Campaign Name:** To Wren and Back

**Start Year:** April 2026

**System:** D&D Lastest edition 5.5

### Books Allowed

Players Handbook (PHB) 2024 - [Beyond Marketplace](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024)

### The City State of Wren

We will spend our adventuring careers perched inside the walls of the City State of Wren. The streets of Wren snake down the foot of the Old Smoking Provider-- Mount Wren to the Harbor of the gray Strait of Mantara. The city state, a bejeweled artifact of an ancient friendship between the Dwarves and Dragonborn unity of clans, houses, and other maintainers of a major trade hub between all peoples of Republics.

 Although all species call the city state home — the streets, walls, docks, and buildings wrought by the stone masons of the Dwarves and Dragonborn. Scaffolding is perpetually hung of cliff side and structures of wren. The craftsman, enlightened, and traders of all the known worlde populace control all power within its walls.

### Major Organizations with-in the City State

The Stronghold of Providers - A major religious organization traditionally built upon the Goddess Wren the Provider of Mists and Smoke. The renown of the Providers of Wren are known far and wide from the lands above and below.

#### Craigorth

A major guild that controls trade between the Harbor, the passages & passes above, below, and abroad with the city-state.

#### The Light of Permethrin

A collective of mystical enlightenment and academia that has brought the people from below and above from that which would bring darkness. One of the corner stone in patron-ship to the ancient discourse between enlightened followers of the God, Scribe, The Many Hands of Enlightenment and Monks of Goatshoem near the pinnacle of Wren.

#### The United of the Muses

Another mystical and spiritual cornerstone of the city state. The Muses are an unfathomable number of Gods and Goddesses enshrined by the institutions that each carry a name of whom provides the breath of life beyond toil, coin,  and bread. The Republics rich history of honoring the communion of people. The worshipful of the Muses are the manifestation of that culture.

#### The Militias and Magistrates of Wren

Not really a faction per se or organization. But a partnership of land owners that mobilizes forces for the common interests of the city state. There is not a city guard per se. "Protection" is provided collectively by land owners.

#### Repoussé of the Landrien

Worshipful of the Goddess Landrea, the pensive and awakened provisioner of dreams. She is also the patron Goddess of Travel, Trade, and Wanderlust. They are the keepers of the Gates of Wren. Each Gate is also a Temple to Landrea.

### Character Entry Point

All characters will start out as members of the House of Roslyn. Session 0 will cover laying the context of what that means as a party. The House Roslyn owns the Palazzo de Roslyn. The palazzo sits atop a major public conveyances deep into the Spirals. The Spirals is the top most layer of the lands below the city state. Those who would bring darkness are at peace and commerce because of the lanterns Roslyn embodies for the republics and city-state.
